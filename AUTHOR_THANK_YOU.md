# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Yuanhe Zhang, Jason D. Lee, and Fanghui Liu for making a large
formal statistical-learning-theory development public, for exposing the
proof structure behind the headline results, and for releasing an artifact
that can be audited at the source, declaration, and full-build levels.

This dossier preserves the authors' work and keeps executable evidence
separate from historical-priority and human-supervision statements that code
alone cannot establish.
